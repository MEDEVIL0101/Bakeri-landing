Partner guidelines

Grow it the right way.

Food makers are a close-knit community. We’d rather grow slowly and keep their trust.

Outreach done right

Cold outreach is welcome when it follows anti-spam law (Canada’s CASL and the US CAN-SPAM Act).

  • Only contact addresses a business publishes itself, about their business.
  • Say who you are and that you’re a Bakerï partner.
  • Make it easy to say no, and never contact a shop again once they do.
  • No bought lists, mass blasts or automated DMs.

Representing Bakerï

You’re an independent partner, so:

  • Disclose that you earn a commission when you recommend Bakerï publicly.
  • Keep claims honest — features and prices as they are on our site.
  • No paid search ads on the Bakerï name, and no ads that look like they’re from us.
  • No self-referrals or signing vendors up on their behalf.

Questions

Good to know.

How is this different from the Referral Program?

The Referral Program is for Bakerï vendors sharing a link with friends, and needs an active Bakerï plan. Community Growth Partners don’t need a shop, can have custom commission terms, and get a full dashboard with campaign tracking, an outreach list and their own payouts.

Do I need a Bakerï account or shop?

No. When you’re approved we set up a partner account for you. If you already sell on Bakerï, your partner dashboard uses the same login.

How do vendors get counted as mine?

When they sign up through your link (we remember it for 30 days in their browser) or type your code at sign-up. The code used at sign-up is what counts.

When and how am I paid?

Each commission clears 4 days after the vendor’s payment, then pays out on the 1st of the month through Stripe, once $20 or more is ready. You’ll connect a bank account through Stripe from your dashboard — it takes about five minutes.

Can my team or brand manager do outreach for me?

Yes. Many partners have someone reaching out to shops on their behalf. They work from your dashboard, under your code, and follow the same guidelines.

What about taxes?

You’re paid as an independent partner, so you’re responsible for reporting the income. Stripe may ask for your tax details when you set up payouts.

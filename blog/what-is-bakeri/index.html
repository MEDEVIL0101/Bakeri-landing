About Bakerï

Bakerï: the all-in-one commerce and business platform for creators and makers

November 9, 2026 · Bakerï team

The way people build businesses has changed. Today, entrepreneurs don’t necessarily need a brick-and-mortar storefront, a warehouse or a traditional retail operation to turn an idea into a business. They can build an audience, create products, sell directly to customers and develop an independent brand from virtually anywhere.

From home-based food businesses and artisan makers to digital creators, specialty product designers and independent entrepreneurs, a new generation of businesses is emerging.

But the technology available to support them remains fragmented. Selling products, managing orders, maintaining customer relationships, creating digital offerings and running day-to-day operations often means juggling multiple platforms and disconnected tools.

Bakerï was created to bring it all together.

Bakerï is an all-in-one creator-commerce and business-management platform designed for independent creators, food entrepreneurs and makers. Combining the best ideas behind platforms like Stan Store, Hotplate, Shopify and Skool, Bakerï provides the infrastructure to build a brand, monetize creativity, manage customers and operate a business from one centralized platform.

Its philosophy is simple: own your audience, own your business and take control of how you sell.

Three ways to sell. One platform.

Bakerï is built around three distinct product categories: Food, Digital and Physical.

Food

For bakers, specialty food producers, confectioners, caterers and independent food entrepreneurs. Users can sell ready-made products, accept preorders, manage custom requests and coordinate their food businesses.

Digital

For creators and educators selling recipes, downloadable resources, courses, guides, memberships and other digital products. Bakerï provides opportunities to monetize knowledge and creativity without relying exclusively on physical inventory.

Physical

For independent makers, artisans, designers and entrepreneurs selling tangible products. From handcrafted goods and specialty merchandise to products that can be packaged and shipped, Bakerï supports businesses selling beyond their immediate geographic market.

Whether someone is selling custom cakes, gourmet treats, digital baking courses, handcrafted accessories or shipped specialty goods, Bakerï provides a common foundation for managing their commercial activities.

More than a storefront

For many independent entrepreneurs, running a business means juggling an assortment of disconnected services. Orders arrive through social media, payments are tracked separately, customer information is scattered across platforms, and business operations rely on spreadsheets and manual processes.

Bakerï brings these activities together.

Beyond branded storefronts and online sales, the platform is designed to support the operational side of entrepreneurship. Order management, customer relationships, recipe management, ingredient costing, scheduling and business organization help users spend less time administering their businesses and more time creating.

Importantly, Bakerï isn’t built around finding customers for its users. It’s built around helping them own the relationships they establish, control their storefronts and build independent businesses without depending entirely on third-party marketplaces or social media algorithms.

Quick answers

What is Bakerï?

Bakerï is an all-in-one creator-commerce and business-management platform for independent creators, food entrepreneurs and makers. It brings together a branded storefront, online sales, order management, customer relationships and business tools such as recipe management, ingredient costing and scheduling.

Who is Bakerï for?

Home-based food businesses, bakers, confectioners, caterers and specialty food producers; creators and educators selling recipes, courses, guides, downloads and memberships; and artisans, designers and makers selling physical products that can be packaged and shipped.

What can you sell on Bakerï?

Three product categories: Food (ready-made products, preorders and custom requests), Digital (recipes, downloadable resources, courses, guides and memberships) and Physical (handcrafted goods, merchandise and shipped products).

Is Bakerï a marketplace?

No. Bakerï isn’t built around finding customers for its users. It gives each business its own storefront and helps them own their customer relationships, instead of depending on third-party marketplaces or social media algorithms.

How much does Bakerï cost?

Starter is $10 per month with a 5% commission on online sales. Pro is $30 per month with no sales commission. Both come with a 7-day free trial. Prices are in USD.

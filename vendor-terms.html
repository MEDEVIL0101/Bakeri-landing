9A. Plans, Free Trial and Billing

9A.1 Plans. To offer Products through a Storefront, the Vendor must hold an active Plan. Plan prices are shown in the Web App before the Vendor subscribes. 9A.2 Payment method. The Vendor authorizes Bakerï, through Stripe, to charge the payment method the Vendor provides for each Plan payment when it falls due, until the Plan is cancelled.

9A.3 Free Trial. A Vendor's first Plan includes a free trial of seven (7) days. A payment method is required to start the Free Trial. Each Vendor, and each payment method, may receive one Free Trial only. Bakerï may end a Free Trial early, and charge the Plan price, where the same payment method has already been used for a Free Trial.

9A.4 Automatic renewal. Unless the Vendor cancels before the Free Trial ends, the Plan price is charged when the Free Trial ends. Each Plan then renews automatically at the end of each billing period, and the then-current Plan price is charged, until the Vendor cancels. Bakerï sends a reminder by email at least three (3) days before a Free Trial ends.

9A.5 Cancellation and changes. The Vendor may cancel or change its Plan at any time in the Web App under Plan & billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and the Plan remains active until then. Payments already made are not refunded, except where required by law. A change between Plans or billing periods may be prorated by Stripe.

9A.6 Vendors from before Plans. A Vendor whose account was created before October 31, 2026 keeps the Starter Plan free of any monthly or yearly charge for as long as its account remains open, with the service fee in Section 9.1. Such a Vendor may subscribe to Pro and, if that subscription ends, returns to the free Starter Plan.

9A.7 Failed payments. If a Plan payment fails, Stripe may retry it. If the payment has not succeeded within three (3) days for a first payment, or seven (7) days for a renewal, the Vendor's Storefront stops accepting Orders until a Plan is active again. The Vendor's account and content are not deleted.

9A.8 Taxes. Plan prices exclude any sales or similar taxes, which are added where Bakerï is required to collect them.

9B. Referral Program

9B.1 Eligibility. A Vendor with an active Plan, including a Free Trial or the free Starter Plan in Section 9A.6, may obtain a referral code and link under Settings, Referral Program.

9B.2 Commission. When a new vendor creates an account using the Vendor's referral code or link, the Vendor earns a commission of twenty percent (20%) of each Plan payment that referred vendor makes, calculated on the amount actually paid before tax, for as long as the referred vendor's Plan remains active. No commission is earned on a Free Trial, on service fees, or on Products sold.

9B.3 Active Plan required. Commission is earned only on Plan payments made while the referring Vendor itself holds an active Plan. A Vendor whose Plan has lapsed earns no commission for that period, including on referred vendors who are still paying.

9B.4 Payment. Commission becomes payable four (4) days after the Plan payment it relates to and is paid monthly, on or about the first day of each month, in US dollars, to the Vendor's Direct Deposit account. Commission accrues while the Vendor has no Direct Deposit and is paid once one is connected.

9B.5 Refunds. If a Plan payment is refunded in full, the commission on it is reversed.

9B.6 Promotion. Referrals must be made organically, such as by sharing a link, a recommendation or a storefront link. Paid advertising using Bakerï's name or the Vendor's referral link is not permitted. Bakerï may disable a referral code and stop further commission where this section is breached.

9B.7 Changing referrer. A referred vendor's referrer may be changed only at that vendor's request, made to hello@bakeriapp.com within forty-five (45) days of the vendor's account being created.

9B.8 Privacy. The referring Vendor sees the number and plan status of its referrals and its own commission totals only, not the referred vendors' sales or payment amounts.

9B.9 Changes. Bakerï may change or end the Referral Program on thirty (30) days' notice. Commission earned before the change takes effect remains payable.

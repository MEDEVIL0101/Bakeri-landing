Your referral link

Two easy ways to share it.

Add your link to your storefront, or copy it straight from your account. Anyone who signs up with it is tracked as your referral automatically.

On your storefront

  1. Copy your link from Settings → Refer a Vendor.
  2. In Storefront, add a Link.
  3. Paste your referral link and give it a title like “Start your own shop on Bakerï”.

Share it directly

  1. Open Settings → Refer a Vendor on the web or in the app.
  2. Find your referral code and link.
  3. Tap Copy link or Share and send it to someone who’d be a great fit.
Commission breakdown

20% of every payment, every month.

You earn 20% of your referral’s plan price each time they pay. It keeps going for as long as their plan stays active.

Prices in USD. Commission is worked out on what your referral actually pays, before tax — so a yearly plan earns 20% of the yearly payment. No commission is earned during a free trial.

Tracking & payouts

Paid out on the 1st.

Follow every referral in Refer a Vendor — who joined, who’s on a free trial, who’s paying — and your pending, ready, and paid totals.

When you earn

Once your referral’s 7-day free trial ends and their plan is paid.

When you’re paid

Monthly, on the 1st — one payout covering everything that has cleared.

Where it goes

Straight to your connected Stripe account. Not connected yet? Your commission is tracked and paid once you are.

Refunds

If a referral’s plan payment is refunded, the commission on it is reversed.

For example
  • Jan 1Your friend signs up with your link and starts their free trial.
  • Jan 8Their trial ends and their first plan payment goes through — you start earning.
  • Feb 1Your commission is paid out to your Stripe account.
Good to know

Keeping it fair for everyone.

Keep your plan active.

You must have an active Bakerï plan to earn referral commission.

  • If you cancel your plan, you stop earning commission — even on referrals who are still subscribed.

Changing referrers

To keep things fair and consistent:

  • Referrers can only be changed within 45 days of the original signup.
  • Changes are only made when the request comes from the referred creator, not the referrer — email hello@bakeriapp.com.
